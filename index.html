Introduction

I am a second-year master's student in the LDS Lab at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). In September 2026 I will begin my PhD in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at USTC and the Shanghai Innovation Institution (SII), advised by Prof. Fuli Feng and Prof. Yixin Cao.

My research focuses on building large language model (LLM) agents that can operate reliably over long horizons and continually improve through their own experience.

Long-Horizon Agents

Agents that stay reliable in unfamiliar, complex environments: active exploration before commitment, building a structured model of the environment and then acting on it, self-verification of intermediate reasoning, and robustness of the grounding such agents depend on.

Self-Evolving Agents & Recursive Self-Improvement

Agents that improve from their own experience: accumulating reusable skills and experience that transfer across tasks, and recursively distilling their own rollouts into a better policy.

Agentic RL & Fine-Tuning

Supervision at the right granularity: upweighting reasoning-critical tokens over boilerplate during fine-tuning, and dense turn-level credit for multi-turn agentic RL.
