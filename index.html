Introduction

My research focuses on developing reliable and self-improving large language model (LLM) agents for long-horizon interactive environments. I study how agents can reason, explore, verify intermediate progress, learn from experience, and remain robust when deployed in complex real-world settings.

LLM-Based Agent Reasoning and Fine-Tuning

Investigating how different tokens contribute to agent fine-tuning, with emphasis on separating critical reasoning tokens from repetitive boilerplate tokens.

Long-Horizon Interactive Agents

Developing methods for autonomous exploration, map-then-act reasoning, self-verification, and experience-driven learning under sparse rewards and expensive rollouts.

Agent Security, Robustness, and Deployment

Studying safety risks in deployed GUI agents, including visual-grounding backdoors and robustness challenges in real-world interfaces.
